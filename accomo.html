Welcome to Hilton Vancouver Metrotown

The Hilton Vancouver Metrotown has been selected as the official venue for the Batang Nichols Reunion: Vancouver, Canada 2027. Whether you're traveling from Canada, the United States, or overseas, staying at the reunion hotel allows you to enjoy every activity without the need to commute.

Located in the heart of Burnaby, British Columbia, the hotel is only a short walk from Metropolis at Metrotown, Crystal Mall, restaurants, cafés, and the Metrotown SkyTrain Station, providing easy access to downtown Vancouver and Vancouver International Airport (YVR).

🎉 Reunion Accommodation Information

The Hilton Vancouver Metrotown is the official venue for the Batang Nichols Reunion: Vancouver, Canada 2027.

Attendees are encouraged to stay at the reunion hotel for maximum convenience and to enjoy more time reconnecting with classmates, batchmates, friends, and neighbors throughout the reunion weekend.

The Organizing Committee is exploring the possibility of securing a special group room rate for reunion attendees. If a group rate becomes available, booking instructions and reservation details will be posted on this page and announced through the Batang Nichols communication channels.

📅 Reservation Reminder

Hotel rooms are subject to availability. We recommend making your reservation as early as possible, especially if you plan to stay during the reunion weekend.

If a reunion room block or special group rate is confirmed, attendees will be notified promptly through the Batang Nichols Reunion website and official announcements.

Early booking is strongly encouraged to ensure your preferred room type and the best available rates.

💡 Helpful Travel Tips

  • Book your hotel accommodations early for the best room availability.
  • Bring comfortable walking shoes if you plan to explore Vancouver.
  • September weather in Metro Vancouver is usually mild, with daytime temperatures ranging from approximately 60°F to 72°F (16°C to 22°C).
  • Public transportation is excellent, and the SkyTrain provides convenient access to many attractions.
  • International visitors should verify passport and travel document requirements before departure.
